Figure 4. Latitude-band decomposition of long-term TCEP intensity trends across the six ocean basins. (a) Western Pacific, (b) Eastern Pacific, (c) North Atlantic, (d) North Indian Ocean, (e) South Indian Ocean, and (f) South Pacific. Bars show total latitude-band contributions (light grey), latitudinal redistribution (muted red), within-band intensity change (muted blue), and residuals (white). Residuals have the definition given in Figure 2. Error bars indicate 95% intervals from 1,000 residual moving-block bootstrap replicates with four-year blocks, and asterisks indicate intervals excluding zero. No contribution is estimated for 35°–90°N in the North Indian Ocean because this combination contains no TCEP events. The dashed horizontal line indicates zero trend; units are mm (3 h)⁻¹ decade⁻¹.
