Figure S1. Conceptual definition of TCEP. Bars represent consecutive 3-hour precipitation observations at a grid cell, and shaded intervals indicate TC-influence windows. Green bars show non-TC precipitation, salmon bars show TC-associated precipitation, and the dashed line marks the local wet-period 99th-percentile threshold (POT99) for 1980–2023. Wet periods have 3-hour precipitation greater than 0.30 mm, equivalent to a mean rate greater than 0.10 mm h⁻¹. Hatched bars identify TC-associated observations that strictly exceed POT99; threshold exceedances outside TC-influence windows do not qualify as TCEP. In the event catalog, all qualifying grid cells associated with the same storm and 3-hour interval form one TCEP event.
