Figure 3. Annual mean TCEP intensity in six tropical cyclone basins during 1980–2023. Panels show the North Indian Ocean (a), Western Pacific (b), Eastern Pacific (c), North Atlantic (d), South Indian Ocean (e), and South Pacific (f). Solid lines and markers show annual values, and dashed lines show Theil–Sen trends. Shading shows 95% confidence bands for the fitted trends from 1,000 residual moving-block bootstrap repetitions using four-year blocks. Panel annotations report trend estimates and two-sided Mann–Kendall p values. Intensity and trend units are mm (3 h)⁻¹ and mm (3 h)⁻¹ decade⁻¹, respectively.
