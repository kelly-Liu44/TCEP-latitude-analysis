Figure S3. Poleward shifts in TC activity and TCEP events during 1980–2023. Panels show annual mean absolute latitude for Northern Hemisphere TC-track records (a), Southern Hemisphere TC-track records (b), Northern Hemisphere TCEP events (c), and Southern Hemisphere TCEP events (d). Markers show annual means, dashed lines show Theil–Sen trends, and shading indicates 95% confidence bands from 1,000 residual moving-block bootstrap repetitions with four-year blocks. Annotations report decadal trends and two-sided Mann–Kendall p values. Positive trends indicate poleward shifts, and latitude is expressed in degrees.
