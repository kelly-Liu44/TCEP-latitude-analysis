Figure S4. Conditional probability-density distributions of TCEP intensity across latitude bands. Panels show the Northern Hemisphere (a) and Southern Hemisphere (b) for 1980–2023. Bars are histograms normalized by the complete storm–3-hour event sample within each latitude band, and curves show Gaussian kernel-density estimates. The displayed range ends at the hemisphere-wide 99th percentile without renormalizing the visible observations. These distributions describe intensity conditional on TCEP occurrence within each band, rather than the relative occurrence frequency among bands. Intensity is expressed in mm (3 h)⁻¹.
