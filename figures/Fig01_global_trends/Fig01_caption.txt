Figure 1. Intensification of TCEP during 1980–2023. (a) Time series of global annual mean storm–3-hour TCEP event intensity. The solid line and markers show annual values, the dashed line indicates the Theil–Sen trend, and shading shows the 95% confidence band for the fitted trend from 1,000 residual moving-block bootstrap replicates with four-year blocks. (b, c) As in (a), but for the Northern and Southern Hemispheres, respectively. (d) Spatial distribution of grid-level trends in TCEP intensity estimated using the Theil–Sen slope; dots indicate trends significant at p < 0.05 based on the Mann–Kendall test. Numbered boxes highlight coherent regions of positive trends over the Arabian Sea and northwestern Indian Ocean (box 1; 15°–30°N, 60°–80°E), western North Atlantic (box 2; 30°–45°N, 70°–40°W), southern tropical Indian Ocean (box 3; 20°S–0°, 40°–80°E), and western North Pacific east of Japan (box 4; 35°–45°N, 140°–170°E). (e) Zonal-mean mapped trend and the corresponding spatial standard deviation across longitudes. Intensity and trend units are mm (3 h)⁻¹ and mm (3 h)⁻¹ decade⁻¹, respectively; TCEP identification uses the local POT99 threshold.
