Figure S5. Land–ocean contrasts in TCEP intensity trends and latitude-band contributions. Panels (a,b) show annual mean intensity of extreme-grid-cell records over land and ocean in the Northern and Southern Hemispheres, respectively, during 1980–2023. Dashed lines show Theil–Sen trends, shading indicates 95% confidence bands from 1,000 residual moving-block bootstrap repetitions with four-year blocks, and annotations report Mann–Kendall p values. Panels (c,d) show latitudinal redistribution contributions (hatched bars) and within-band intensity contributions (solid bars) for the two hemispheres. Surface type is determined by precipitation-grid location, whereas hemisphere and latitude band follow storm-center latitude. This analysis weights extreme-grid-cell records equally, whereas the main event analysis weights storm–3-hour events equally. Intensity and trend units are mm (3 h)⁻¹ and mm (3 h)⁻¹ decade⁻¹, respectively.
