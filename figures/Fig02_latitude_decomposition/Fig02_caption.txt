Figure 2. Latitudinal decomposition of TCEP intensity trends. Bars show total latitude-band contributions and the corresponding redistribution, within-band intensity, and residual contributions for (a) the Northern Hemisphere and (b) the Southern Hemisphere during 1980–2023. Error bars denote 95% intervals from 1,000 residual moving-block bootstrap replicates with four-year blocks, and asterisks indicate intervals excluding zero. A positive redistribution contribution in an individual band indicates an increase in that band’s relative event frequency, weighted by its climatological mean intensity. Positive within-band contributions indicate increasing mean event intensity within fixed latitude bands. The residual equals the total band slope minus the redistribution and within-band slopes and includes interaction effects and Theil–Sen non-additivity. Units are mm (3 h)⁻¹ decade⁻¹.
