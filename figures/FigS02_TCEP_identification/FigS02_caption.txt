Figure S2. TCPF and TCEP identification for Walaka at 03:00 UTC on 3 October 2018. The example uses MSWEP V2.8 precipitation (Beck et al., 2019) and IBTrACS V4 storm positions (Knapp et al., 2010), with the storm center at 16.75°N, 170.20°W. Colors show 3-hour precipitation in TC-attributed grid cells, and cyan contours outline cells that strictly exceed their local POT99 thresholds. Wet cells with precipitation greater than 0.30 mm are grouped using four-neighbor connectivity within 1,000 km of the center; features are retained when their centroids lie within 500 km (Jiang et al., 2011; Guzman & Jiang, 2021). The black cross marks the storm center, and dashed circles indicate the 500-km and 1,000-km radii. The event contains 1,393 extreme grid cells and has a mean intensity of 13.53 mm (3 h)⁻¹.
